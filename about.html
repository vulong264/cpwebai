§ 01  /  ORIGIN

We started in 2020 building real products, not selling timesheets.

CoderPush began as a product studio in Ho Chi Minh City. Our first paying year was spent shipping consumer apps under our own brand — learning what production actually costs when you’re both the engineer and the customer.

That backbone never left. Today we run two AI products of our own — Presight, a governed AI Data Analyst, and Promvis, Vietnam’s financial data infrastructure rebuilt for the AI era. Everything we sell to clients, we’ve already shipped on our own platforms first.

We’re an AWS Advanced Partner with the Generative AI Software Competency — one of a small set of firms in Southeast Asia certified to build AI in production for regulated industries.

§ 03  /  PRINCIPLES

Six things we actually believe.

▸ 01

Ship our own before we sell yours.

We don’t take an AI engagement on unless we’ve solved the analogous problem on Presight or Promvis. Our products are our R&D.

▸ 02

The model is rarely the hard part.

Compliance gates, eval suites, deployment topology, audit logs — this is where projects fail. We design backwards from production, not from the demo.

▸ 03

Senior engineers, or none.

Every engagement starts with a senior engineer reading your problem — no SDR, no junior bait-and-switch. If we can’t staff senior, we tell you.

▸ 04

VPC by default. Customer data, customer perimeter.

Every banking client we work with runs inference inside their AWS account or on-prem. Your data is yours. The audit trail is yours.

▸ 05

Postmortems, not pitch decks.

Our case studies name engineers, list trade-offs, and admit what we’d do differently. The same writing standard applies internally.

▸ 06

Walk away if we’re wrong for the job.

If a project needs a research lab, an in-house team, or a different vendor, we say so on the first call. Long-term reputation beats this quarter’s booking.
